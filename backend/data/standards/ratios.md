# Georgia Standards of Excellence (MGSE) — Ratios & Proportional Relationships

## Grade 5
### MGSE5.OA.3
Generate two numerical patterns using two given rules, identify relationships between corresponding terms, and graph ordered pairs on a coordinate plane.
- Summary: Lay the foundational groundwork for ratios and tables of equivalent pairs by creating sequences of numbers governed by rules (e.g., add 3, add 6) and observing constant relationships between them.
- Key concepts: numerical patterns, corresponding terms, proportional tables, coordinate graphing.

### MGSE5.NF.5
Interpret multiplication as scaling (resizing), comparing the size of a product to the size of one factor without performing arithmetic.
- Summary: Understand that multiplying by a fraction greater than 1 increases quantity, while multiplying by a fraction less than 1 decreases quantity. This is the direct conceptual precursor to ratio scaling and scale factors.
- Key concepts: scaling up, scaling down, scale factor, multiplier comparison.

## Grade 6
### MGSE6.RP.1
Understand the concept of a ratio and use ratio language to describe a multiplicative comparison between two quantities.
- Summary: A ratio compares two quantities (part-to-part or part-to-whole). Ratios can be expressed with words ("3 to 2"), a colon ("3:2"), or as a fraction ("3/2").
- Key concepts: ratio comparison, part-to-part, part-to-whole, ratio notation.

### MGSE6.RP.2
Understand the concept of a unit rate a/b associated with a ratio a:b with b != 0, and use rate language in real-world contexts.
- Summary: A unit rate describes how much of one quantity corresponds to 1 unit of another quantity (e.g., miles per hour, price per ounce, heartbeats per minute).
- Key concepts: unit rate, per unit, dividing numerator by denominator.
- Formula/Rule: Unit rate = Quantity A / Quantity B.

### MGSE6.RP.3
Use ratio and rate reasoning to solve real-world and mathematical problems using tables of equivalent ratios, tape diagrams, double number line diagrams, or cross-multiplication equations.
- Summary: Find unknown quantities in proportions by creating equivalent ratio tables, finding the scale factor between rows, and solving unit pricing and speed problems.
- Key concepts: equivalent ratios, cross multiplication, tape diagrams, unit price comparison.
- Formula/Rule: a/b = c/d => a*d = b*c.
